Research

What I work on.

Optics
Refractive index sensing and ray deflection imaging, which read the composition of a mixture at the boundary between gas and liquid without putting anything into the fluid. A submerged mirror lengthens the optical path until the dissolution front becomes visible to the camera.
Transport
Diffusion and mass transfer coefficients for low GWP refrigerants such as propane (R290) and R1234yf, dissolving into PAG and POE compressor lubricants under pressure.
Simulation
Molecular dynamics in LAMMPS, run to explain why a refrigerant and an oil take up less volume mixed together than they did apart.
